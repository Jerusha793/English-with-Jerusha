Understanding IS

What is IS?

IS is used for one person, one place, or one thing.

  • ✅ He is happy.
  • ✅ The school is big.
  • ✅ My pen is blue.

Structure:

Subject + IS + Complement

తెలుగు: ఉన్నాడు / ఉంది / ఉన్నది
